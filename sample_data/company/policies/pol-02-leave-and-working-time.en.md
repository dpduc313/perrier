## POL-02 §1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets working hours (thời giờ làm việc), rest periods (thời giờ nghỉ ngơi), public holidays (nghỉ lễ, Tết), annual leave (nghỉ phép năm), other paid and unpaid leave, overtime (làm thêm giờ) and remote work (làm việc từ xa). It applies to every employee with a labour contract, including employees on probation. How leave and overtime are paid is in POL-01 Compensation and Benefits Policy.

## POL-02 §2. Working hours

- The working week is Monday to Friday, 8 hours a day and 40 hours a week. Saturday and Sunday are the weekly days off (ngày nghỉ hằng tuần).
- Core hours are 8:30 to 17:30, with a lunch break from 12:00 to 13:00 that is not working time.
- With their manager's agreement, an employee may start between 8:00 and 9:30 and finish 9 hours later (flexible hours, giờ làm việc linh hoạt).
- Employees record attendance (chấm công) with their access card at the office, or in the HR system when working remotely.

Arriving more than 15 minutes after the agreed start time without notice counts as late arrival (đi muộn). Being late more than three times in a month is a violation of labour discipline under POL-03 §3.

## POL-02 §3. Public holidays

Employees are off with full pay on the public holidays of Article 112 of the Labour Code 2019, 11 days a year:

| Holiday | Days |
|---|---|
| New Year's Day (Tết Dương lịch), 1 January | 1 |
| Lunar New Year (Tết Âm lịch) | 5 |
| Hùng Kings' Commemoration Day (Giỗ Tổ Hùng Vương), 10th day of the 3rd lunar month | 1 |
| Reunification Day (Ngày Chiến thắng), 30 April | 1 |
| International Labour Day (Ngày Quốc tế Lao động), 1 May | 1 |
| National Day (Quốc khánh), 2 September and one adjacent day | 2 |

The Lunar New Year holiday runs for five working days starting the day before the first day of Tết. A holiday that falls on a weekend is moved to the next working day. Each year the HR Department publishes the exact dates following the Government's announcement. Public holidays are not counted as working days when computing the standard working days of a month (POL-01 §3).

## POL-02 §4. Annual leave

1. **Entitlement:** 12 working days of paid annual leave (nghỉ phép năm) per calendar year, plus one day for every full five years of service (thâm niên) at the Company, under Articles 113 and 114 of the Labour Code 2019.
2. **First year:** an employee who joins during the year accrues one day for each full month worked. Employees on probation accrue leave but may use it only after probation ends.
3. **Requests:** leave is requested in the HR system and approved by the direct manager:
   - at least 3 working days in advance for leave of up to 2 days;
   - at least 2 weeks in advance for leave of 3 days or more.
4. **Carry-over:** unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March; after that they lapse, unless the Company refused the leave for business reasons.
5. **On leaving the Company:** unused annual leave is paid at the employee's base salary.

## POL-02 §5. Other leave

**Paid personal leave (nghỉ việc riêng có hưởng lương)**, under Article 115 of the Labour Code 2019:

- own marriage: 3 days;
- marriage of a child: 1 day;
- death of a parent, parent-in-law, spouse or child: 3 days.

**Sick leave (nghỉ ốm).** The employee informs their manager on the first day and submits the medical certificate (giấy chứng nhận nghỉ việc hưởng BHXH) within 5 working days of returning. The days are not paid by the Company; the employee receives the sickness benefit from social insurance (BHXH), which the HR Department claims on their behalf.

**Maternity and paternity leave (nghỉ thai sản).** Female employees receive six months of maternity leave. Male employees receive 5 to 14 working days when their wife gives birth, depending on the kind of birth. These days are paid by social insurance under the Social Insurance Law.

**Unpaid leave (nghỉ không lương).** Up to 30 days a year with the approval of the department head, and longer with the approval of the General Director. Unpaid days are deducted from the salary in proportion (POL-01 §3). If an employee works fewer than 14 days in a month, no social insurance is paid for that month (POL-01 §8), which can affect their insurance benefits.

**Unauthorised absence (nghỉ không phép).** Absence without approval is unpaid and is a violation under POL-03 §3. Five cumulative days of unauthorised absence in 30 days, or 20 days in 365 days, may lead to dismissal under Article 125 of the Labour Code 2019.

## POL-02 §6. Overtime

1. Overtime must be requested by the manager and agreed by the employee in advance, in the HR system. Overtime that was not approved in advance is not paid.
2. Overtime may not exceed 50% of normal working hours in a day, 40 hours in a month, or 200 hours in a year, under Article 107 of the Labour Code 2019.
3. Overtime is paid at 150% on weekdays, 200% on weekly days off and 300% on public holidays (POL-01 §5).
4. Team leads and above (G5 to G7) manage their own time and are not paid for overtime, except for overtime on public holidays approved by the General Director.

## POL-02 §7. Remote work

Employees may work remotely (làm việc từ xa) up to 2 days a week with their manager's approval, when their work does not need them in the office. Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, use only Company devices and the Company VPN, and follow the information security rules in POL-03 §6. Employees on probation work from the office.

## POL-02 §8. Resignation notice

An employee who resigns gives written notice (báo trước) before their last day, under Article 35 of the Labour Code 2019:

- 45 days for an indefinite-term contract (hợp đồng không xác định thời hạn);
- 30 days for a fixed-term contract of 12 to 36 months;
- 3 working days during probation.

Leaving without the required notice (nghỉ việc không báo trước) is a violation under POL-03 §3, and under Article 40 of the Labour Code 2019 the employee must pay the Company half a month's base salary plus the salary for the days of notice not given, and loses the severance allowance (trợ cấp thôi việc).
