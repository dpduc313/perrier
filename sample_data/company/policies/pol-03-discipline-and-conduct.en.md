This policy is the Company's labour regulations (nội quy lao động) under Article 118 of the Labour Code 2019.

## POL-03 §1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the conduct the Company expects, what counts as a violation of labour discipline (vi phạm kỷ luật lao động), the disciplinary actions (hình thức xử lý kỷ luật lao động) the Company may take, compensation for damage to Company property (bồi thường thiệt hại), and the procedure for all of these. It applies to every employee, including employees on probation and managers.

## POL-03 §2. Principles

1. **No fines.** The Company never fines employees or cuts their salary as a punishment (phạt tiền, cắt lương), which Article 127 of the Labour Code 2019 forbids. The only money an employee may have to pay is compensation for damage under §5.
2. **One action per violation.** A single violation receives one disciplinary action. If an employee commits several violations at once, only the action for the most serious one applies.
3. **Fair process.** No disciplinary action is taken without the procedure in §8, and the employee always has the right to explain and to be represented.
4. **No discipline** of an employee on sick leave, on approved leave, held by the authorities, or pregnant or on maternity leave, until that situation ends.

## POL-03 §3. Violations of labour discipline

The following are violations. The list gives examples, not every case.

**Minor violations:**

- being late more than three times in a month (POL-02 §2) (đi muộn nhiều lần trong tháng);
- not following an approval process, for example committing spending or signing a document without the required approval (không tuân thủ quy trình phê duyệt);
- leaving without the notice required by POL-02 §8 (nghỉ việc không báo trước);
- unauthorised absence of less than five days (POL-02 §5);
- breaching the information security rules of §6 where no Company or customer data leaks (vi phạm quy định bảo mật thông tin).

**Serious violations:**

- leaking confidential Company or customer data, or source code;
- theft, fraud, embezzlement, gambling, or using or dealing drugs at work;
- sexual harassment in the workplace (quấy rối tình dục tại nơi làm việc) (§7);
- unauthorised absence of five cumulative days in 30 days, or 20 cumulative days in 365 days;
- repeating a violation while under the action "extension of the salary raise period" or "demotion".

## POL-03 §4. Disciplinary actions

Article 124 of the Labour Code 2019 allows four disciplinary actions. The Company applies them as follows:

| Action | When the Company applies it | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Reprimand (khiển trách) | A first minor violation | A written reprimand in the personnel file. No effect on pay. |
| Extension of the salary raise period (kéo dài thời hạn nâng lương) | A minor violation repeated within 12 months of a reprimand | For six months from the decision, the employee receives no salary raise. An April raise (POL-01 §6) falling within those six months is cancelled for that year, not postponed. |
| Demotion (cách chức) | A violation by a manager (G5 to G7) that harms their team or the Company | Removal from the management position and its position allowance (POL-01 §4). |
| Dismissal (sa thải) | A serious violation listed in §3 | The labour contract ends. Only in the cases of Article 125 of the Labour Code 2019. |

**Clearing the record (xóa kỷ luật).** A reprimand is cleared after 3 months, and an extension of the salary raise period after 6 months, if the employee commits no new violation in that time (Article 126). A cleared action no longer counts as an earlier violation.

## POL-03 §5. Compensation for damage to property

An employee who, through negligence, damages or loses Company property, for example a laptop, a monitor, an access card or other equipment, compensates the Company under Article 129 of the Labour Code 2019:

1. The amount is set from the repair cost or the remaining value of the property, and agreed in writing with the employee.
2. Damage worth no more than 10 times the region I minimum wage, caused by negligence, is compensated at no more than 3 months' salary.
3. The compensation is deducted from monthly salary (khấu trừ vào lương). Each month's deduction is at most 30% of that month's salary, and the balance carries to the next months.
4. Compensation is a deduction from net pay (POL-01 §10). It is not a disciplinary action, and does not affect raises or bonuses.

Damage caused deliberately, or the loss of confidential data, is a serious violation under §3 in addition to the compensation.

## POL-03 §6. Information security

- Use only Company devices and accounts for Company work, and keep Company and customer data on Company systems.
- Lock your screen when away from your desk; never share your password or access card.
- Connect from outside the office only through the Company VPN (POL-02 §7).
- Do not paste source code, customer data or personal data of colleagues (dữ liệu cá nhân) into external tools or AI services not approved by the Infrastructure and Operations Department.
- Report a lost device or a suspected leak to it-security@perrytech.example within 24 hours.

## POL-03 §7. Conduct at work

- **Conflict of interest (xung đột lợi ích):** declare in writing to the HR Department any interest in a customer, supplier or competitor, including those of close family members.
- **Gifts (quà tặng):** gifts from customers or suppliers worth more than 500,000 VND must be declared to your department head and, where possible, returned. Cash gifts are never accepted.
- **Harassment and discrimination:** the Company does not tolerate harassment, including sexual harassment, or discrimination for gender, age, origin, religion, disability or any other reason.
- **Company assets:** use Company equipment with care and for work. Personal use of laptops is allowed within reason, with no installation of unlicensed software.

## POL-03 §8. Disciplinary procedure

1. The direct manager or the HR Department records the violation in writing with its evidence.
2. The HR Department invites the employee, at least 5 working days ahead, to a disciplinary meeting with a representative of the Company trade union. The employee may bring a lawyer or another representative.
3. The meeting is recorded in minutes (biên bản), signed by everyone present.
4. The General Director, or a person authorised by them, issues the disciplinary decision (quyết định kỷ luật) and sends it to the employee and the trade union.

**Time limit (thời hiệu).** Discipline must be decided within 6 months of the violation, or 12 months for violations involving finance, property, or the disclosure of technology or business secrets (Article 123).

## POL-03 §9. Reporting a concern

Anyone may report a violation of this policy, in confidence, to their department head, the HR Department (hr@perrytech.example), or the ethics channel (ethics@perrytech.example). The Company does not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## POL-03 §10. Appeals

An employee who disagrees with a disciplinary decision or a compensation amount may complain to the General Director within 15 days of receiving it, or follow the labour dispute procedures of the Labour Code 2019.
