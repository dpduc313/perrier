## POL-01 §1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets how the Company pays its employees: base salary (lương cơ bản), allowances (phụ cấp), bonuses (thưởng), the annual salary review, promotion, and the social insurance and personal income tax deducted from pay. It applies to every employee with a labour contract (hợp đồng lao động) at the Hà Nội and Hồ Chí Minh City offices, including employees on probation (thử việc). It does not apply to freelancers or service contractors.

Working time, leave and overtime approval are in POL-02 Leave and Working Time Policy. Disciplinary actions that affect pay are in POL-03 Discipline and Code of Conduct Policy.

## POL-01 §2. Job grades and salary bands

Every position belongs to one job grade (cấp bậc). Each grade has a monthly salary band (khung lương). An employee's base salary must stay within the band of their grade.

| Grade | Name | Minimum (VND/month) | Maximum (VND/month) |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Fresher (Nhân viên mới) | 9,000,000 | 14,000,000 |
| G2 | Junior (Nhân viên) | 13,000,000 | 20,000,000 |
| G3 | Middle (Nhân viên chính) | 19,000,000 | 30,000,000 |
| G4 | Senior (Chuyên viên cao cấp) | 28,000,000 | 45,000,000 |
| G5 | Team Lead (Trưởng nhóm) | 40,000,000 | 60,000,000 |
| G6 | Department Head (Trưởng phòng) | 55,000,000 | 85,000,000 |
| G7 | Director (Giám đốc) | 90,000,000 | 150,000,000 |

The bands overlap on purpose, so that a promotion does not always need a large raise.

## POL-01 §3. Base salary and probation

The base salary is agreed in the labour contract and stated as a gross monthly amount in Vietnamese đồng (VND).

Probation lasts 60 days for positions that require a college degree (cao đẳng) or higher, which covers every position at the Company. During probation the employee receives 85% of the agreed base salary (lương thử việc), the legal minimum under Article 26 of the Labour Code 2019 (Bộ luật Lao động 2019). From the first day of the month after probation ends, the employee receives the full base salary.

Salary for a month is paid in proportion to the days worked:

> salary pay = base salary × days worked ÷ standard working days of the month

The standard working days (ngày công chuẩn) are the weekdays of the month less public holidays. Days worked exclude unpaid leave (nghỉ không lương) and days before joining or after leaving. Salary for each month is paid by bank transfer by the 5th day of the following month, with a payslip (phiếu lương) sent by email.

## POL-01 §4. Allowances

1. **Meal allowance (phụ cấp ăn trưa):** 730,000 VND per month for every employee, paid in proportion to days worked.
2. **Position allowance (phụ cấp chức vụ):** paid to managers, in proportion to days worked:
   - G5 Team Lead: 3,000,000 VND per month
   - G6 Department Head: 5,000,000 VND per month
   - G7 Director: 10,000,000 VND per month

The position allowance is part of the salary on which social insurance is paid (§8). The meal allowance is not.

## POL-01 §5. Overtime pay

Overtime (làm thêm giờ) must be approved in advance under POL-02 §6. Approved overtime on a weekday is paid at 150% of the hourly rate:

> overtime pay = base salary ÷ standard working days ÷ 8 × 150% × overtime hours

Overtime on a weekly day off is paid at 200%, and on a public holiday at 300%, as required by Article 98 of the Labour Code 2019.

## POL-01 §6. Performance reviews, annual salary review and promotion

**Performance reviews (đánh giá hiệu suất).** Every employee is reviewed twice a year:

- the annual review in January, for the previous calendar year;
- the mid-year review in July, for January to June (period "H1").

The manager gives a rating (xếp loại) from 1 to 5:

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding (Xuất sắc) |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations (Vượt mong đợi) |
| 3 | Meets expectations (Đạt yêu cầu) |
| 2 | Needs improvement (Cần cải thiện) |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory (Không đạt) |

**Annual salary review (điều chỉnh lương định kỳ).** On 1 April, base salaries are raised according to the rating from the January review:

| Rating | Raise |
|---|---|
| 5 | 12% to 15% |
| 4 | 8% to 10% |
| 3 | 4% to 6% |
| 2 | 0% to 2% |
| 1 | No raise |

A raise never takes the salary above the maximum of the employee's grade band (§2). An employee with no January rating (for example, hired after the review) gets no raise that year. An employee under the disciplinary action "extension of the salary raise period" (kéo dài thời hạn nâng lương) in the six months before 1 April gets no raise that year (POL-03 §4).

**Promotion (thăng chức).** On 1 July, an employee at grade G1 to G3 is promoted one grade if their mid-year rating is 5 and their previous annual rating is 4 or 5. The promoted employee's salary rises by 12%, and at least to the minimum of the new band. Promotion to G5 and above is decided by the General Director when a management position is open.

## POL-01 §7. Bonuses

1. **Tết bonus (thưởng Tết):** paid with the February salary, as a number of months of base salary set by the previous year's annual rating:

   | Rating | Tết bonus |
   |---|---|
   | 5 | 2 months |
   | 4 | 1.5 months |
   | 3 | 1 month |
   | 2 | 1 month |
   | 1 | 0.5 month |

   An employee who joined during the previous year receives a share in proportion to the days employed that year. An employee with no rating is treated as rating 3. Employees who joined after 31 December of the previous year receive no Tết bonus.

2. **Quarterly sales bonus (thưởng doanh số quý):** for Sales Department (Phòng Kinh doanh) staff employed since the start of the quarter. It is paid with the salary of the first month of the next quarter:

   | Sales target achieved | Bonus |
   |---|---|
   | Below 80% | None |
   | 80% to 99% | 30% of one month's base salary |
   | 100% to 119% | 50% of one month's base salary |
   | 120% or more | 70% of one month's base salary |

3. **Project bonus (thưởng dự án):** from 3,000,000 to 15,000,000 VND per person, for members of the Software Development and Infrastructure and Operations departments when a project is delivered. The department head proposes the team and amounts; the General Director approves them.

4. **Referral bonus (thưởng giới thiệu ứng viên):** 5,000,000 VND to an employee who referred a candidate who is hired and completes probation. It is paid with the salary of the month the probation ends, if both are still employed then.

All bonuses are part of taxable income (§9).

## POL-01 §8. Social, health and unemployment insurance

The Company registers every employee for compulsory social insurance (bảo hiểm xã hội, BHXH), health insurance (bảo hiểm y tế, BHYT) and unemployment insurance (bảo hiểm thất nghiệp, BHTN). The salary for insurance (lương đóng bảo hiểm) is the base salary plus the position allowance.

| Insurance | Employee pays | Company pays | Salary cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| BHXH | 8% | 17.5% | 20 × the base reference salary (lương cơ sở) |
| BHYT | 1.5% | 3% | 20 × the base reference salary |
| BHTN | 1% | 1% | 20 × the region I minimum wage (lương tối thiểu vùng I) |

The employee's share is deducted from pay. Each rate applies only up to its cap: from 1 July 2026, with a base reference salary of 2,530,000 VND, the BHXH and BHYT cap is 50,600,000 VND; from 1 January 2026, with a region I minimum wage of 5,310,000 VND, the BHTN cap is 106,200,000 VND.

No insurance is paid for a month in which the employee works fewer than 14 days, for example because of long unpaid leave.

## POL-01 §9. Personal income tax

The Company withholds personal income tax (thuế thu nhập cá nhân, TNCN) every month. Taxable income is gross income (salary, allowances, overtime pay and bonuses) less the employee's insurance, the personal deduction (giảm trừ gia cảnh bản thân) of 15,500,000 VND per month, and 6,200,000 VND per month for each registered dependent (người phụ thuộc). From the 2026 tax period, tax is computed on five progressive brackets from 5% to 35% under the Personal Income Tax Law 109/2025/QH15.

To claim a dependent, the employee submits the registration form and supporting documents to the HR Department; the deduction applies from the month they are received.

## POL-01 §10. Net pay and questions

> net pay = gross income − employee insurance − personal income tax − deductions under POL-03 §5

Questions about a payslip go to the HR Department (hr@perrytech.example) within 30 days of the payment date. Errors in the Company's favour are corrected in the next payroll.
